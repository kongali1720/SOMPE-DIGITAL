# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SOMPE-DIGITAL is committed to creating an open, respectful, inclusive, and
constructive environment for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives.
- Focus on ideas and technical merit.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Avoid harassment and discrimination.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment
- Threats
- Discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Deliberate disruption
- Unauthorized disclosure of private information
- Malicious or abusive activity

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when behavior violates this
code of conduct, including warnings, moderation, or removal from project
activities.

The goal is to maintain a healthy environment for collaboration and
innovation.
